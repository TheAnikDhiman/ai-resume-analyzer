"The system gives two scores:
 1. Overall ATS Score — based on all 40+ skills in our database
 2. JD Match Score — based only on skills the specific job requires

 This is intentional. A resume strong in ML might score low
 on an ATS designed for a web developer role, and vice versa.
 Our system shows both so the user gets the complete picture."
